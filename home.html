About

How I work

I've always been drawn to solutioning — when something is broken or inefficient, I want to understand why and build the fix, not just describe it. That instinct is what pulled me into product: it's the role where curiosity about a problem turns into something real that people use.

I'm a Product Analyst in JPMorgan Chase's Innovation Development Program. I started in C&SI Lending, where I co-built a product that became part of the team's workflow, and now work in CRE Lending on AI tools for bankers. Before that, I spent a summer in the Corporate Analyst Development Program supporting risk and controls reviews.
