About

How I work

I've always been drawn to solutioning — when something is broken or inefficient, I want to understand why and build the fix, not just describe it. That instinct is what pulled me into product management: it's the role where curiosity about a problem turns into something real that people use.

At JPMorgan Chase, I've gone from an intern to a full-time PM working on AI products and Commercial Real Estate (CRE) tools, after starting out improving how the firm processes risk. I'm still early in my career, and I like it that way — every project is a chance to learn a new corner of the problem.
