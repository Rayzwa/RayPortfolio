GrowingED was built at OwlHack, Temple University's hackathon, in September 2023: an educational platform for connecting mentees with mentors.

People looking to learn need a way to find mentors, in a learning environment that feels inclusive.

I co-defined the product vision and scoped the MVP features with my teammates.

  • Defined the vision: a platform matching mentees with mentors in an inclusive learning environment.
  • Scoped the MVP so a working product could ship within the hackathon timeline.
  • Integrated Auth0 for secure sign-in.

We shipped a working website within the hackathon and won Best Use of Auth0.
