Momentum was built at an internal JPMorgan Chase hackathon in June 2026. The goal was to define and prototype an AI tool, taking it from a problem statement to a working demo within the event.

Commitments made at work easily get lost amid meetings and competing priorities. Momentum set out to keep those commitments from slipping through the cracks.

I was the product owner, leading a cross-functional team of 10 from problem framing to demo.

  • Framed the user problem the tool would solve.
  • Scoped the concept to what could be defined and prototyped within the hackathon.
  • Shaped the narrative from the problem statement through to the demo.
  • Used mock data so the prototype could be demonstrated end to end.

The team defined and prototyped Momentum and presented it as a demo, with a clear story running from the problem to the working concept.
